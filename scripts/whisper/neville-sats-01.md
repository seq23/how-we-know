## Segment 1 · quotes

An hour of Neville Goddard's state akin to sleep, whispered slowly, to help you fall asleep.

In Feeling Is the Secret, Neville wrote this. “It is in sleep and in prayer, a state akin to sleep, that man enters the subconscious to make his impressions and receive his instructions.”

Neville put it this way, in Out of This World. “Induce a state akin to sleep, a drowsy state, but one in which you are still able to control the direction of your thoughts.”
