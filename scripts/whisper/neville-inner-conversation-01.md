## Segment 1 · explain

An hour of the whisper method, from Neville Goddard's inner conversation, whispered slowly, to help you fall asleep.

Lie back now and let your whole body grow heavy against the mattress. The day is behind you and nothing more needs to be done. Listen close while we talk about a simple way to rest your mind. A teacher named Neville Goddard noticed something very quiet about how we live. He taught that the silent words you say to yourself all day long shape how you see your life. We talk to ourselves from morning until night without even knowing we are doing it.

Most of the time those inner words just repeat old worries and familiar habits. Neville asked people to simply notice that inner talk. He suggested you can change those quiet words so they match a wish already fulfilled. You do not need to force anything or try too hard. Just let your thoughts shift toward a feeling of quiet completion.

Many people today use a whisper method built on this very idea. It is a gentle way to settle your thoughts before sleep takes over. You quietly hear, in your mind, the words you would hear if the wish were already true. Imagine a friendly voice speaking right next to your ear. What would you love to hear someone say when all is well.

Keep it very simple as you lie here in the dark. Find one short and warm sentence that feels right to you. Hear it as if a friend just whispered it to you in the room. Let that phrase repeat again and again in your mind. There is no right or wrong way to let the words drift by.

You just listen to the quiet phrase like a soft song playing in the distance. As you hear it over and over, your body can let go even more. Feel the space around you expanding with every gentle breath you take. The words become a soft pillow for your tired mind to rest upon. You do not have to make anything happen or chase any goal right now.

Just let the simple sentence fold itself into the quiet rhythm of your breathing. Hear it clearly and then let it fade away for a moment. Then let it return just as softly as before. There is nothing else to figure out and nowhere else you need to be. The gentle repetition carries you closer and closer to sleep.

Notice how warm your hands and feet feel as you listen to the phrase. Let every muscle in your face smooth out and soften completely. The day has ended and your mind can finally rest in this quiet space. Just stay with the rhythm of the comforting words in your imagination. Let them stay with you as the edges of sleep begin to blur. Allow yourself to drift further and further down into peace.
