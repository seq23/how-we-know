# 8–12 Minute Deep-Sea Script Template

## Metadata header
- Query:
- Canonical article path:
- Canonical record review date:
- Target runtime:
- Narrator profile version:
- Rights manifest batch:

## 0:00–0:20 — Exact-question hook
State the query exactly. Give the canonical direct answer without adding unsupported drama.

## 0:20–1:15 — Why the question exists
Explain the image, viral clip, or intuition that makes people ask it. Do not invent a news event.

## 1:15–3:30 — Environmental constraint
Establish light, pressure, temperature, food, depth and habitat using the source registry.

## 3:30–6:30 — Adaptation mechanism
Explain two to four body or behavior traits. Pair each claim with a planned visual and source ID.

## 6:30–8:30 — Comparison or myth correction
Contrast surface and deep-sea strategies. Correct the most common overstatement.

## 8:30–10:30 — Named examples
Use two or three creature records. Do not turn a group-level claim into a species-level fact.

## Final 20 seconds — Close and reciprocal link
Restate the answer in fresh language. Direct the viewer to the canonical article for sources and further reading.

## Human review gate
Before voice generation, compare every factual sentence with `content/questions.json`, source IDs, final visual rights records and the article page. The final script—not an earlier draft—becomes the transcript source.
