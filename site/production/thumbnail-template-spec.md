# Thumbnail Template Specification

## Canvas
1280×720, 16:9, under the current YouTube upload limit.

## Stable system
- One dominant creature or depth object.
- Dark abyssal field with one cyan-blue light source.
- Maximum four words of display text.
- High contrast at 10% scale.
- No fake scale, fake teeth, invented eyes, or misleading compositing.

## Query relationship
The video title carries the exact query. Thumbnail text supplies tension or the mechanism, not a duplicate title.

Examples:
- Title: Why Are Deep Sea Creatures Red?
- Thumbnail: INVISIBLE IN RED

## Template layers
1. Background gradient.
2. Licensed subject cutout or frame.
3. Rim light and local contrast.
4. Text block.
5. Small channel mark.
6. Export-safe margin guide.

Every source image must have an admitted asset-rights record.
