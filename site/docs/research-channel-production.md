# Channel production research ledger

Checked 2026-07-30.

- Kokoro official repository: Apache-2.0 inference library and Apache-licensed weights; selected voice configuration is `af_heart`.
- FFmpeg official legal page: LGPL 2.1+ by default, with GPL terms applying when GPL components are enabled. The repository does not redistribute FFmpeg.
- YouTube altered/synthetic-content guidance: creators must review whether realistic synthetic or meaningfully altered content requires disclosure during upload.
- YouTube monetization guidance: reused material must add significant original commentary, transformation, education, or entertainment value. The production contract makes the sourced narration, structure, diagrams, editing, and analysis the primary value.
- NOAA media guidance: federal works are often public domain, but individual credits and third-party material must still be checked. NOAA videos can contain third-party footage.
- Cloudflare TanStack Start guide: current deployment target is Workers with `.output/server/index.mjs` and `.output/public`, deployed through Wrangler.

The candidate-source registry remains conditional. No external footage is approved in the rights manifest merely because its host is a government agency, research institute, or stock library.
