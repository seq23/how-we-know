## Rights research

Source organizations cannot be treated as one interchangeable footage pool:

- NOAA-created material is often reusable, but individual credits, embedded metadata, and third-party footage must be checked.
- MBARI footage is not automatically cleared for commercial use and may require permission or a license.
- Schmidt Ocean Institute's streamlined footage lane is generally noncommercial Creative Commons and is not automatically suitable for a monetized channel.
- Pexels and Pixabay permit broad commercial use under their custom licenses, subject to prohibited-use and third-party-rights limits.
- YouTube Audio Library terms are track-specific; attribution must be retained when required.

No footage has been admitted yet. The empty rights manifest is intentional and prevents source-brand assumptions from becoming license claims.
