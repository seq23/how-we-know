## Why it is built this way

- **No "welcome to my channel," no "don't forget to subscribe."** Both signal amateur
  and both cost retention in the first five seconds, which is the only part most
  viewers watch.
- **Four consecutive questions, unanswered.** The trailer sells the *method*. A
  viewer who finds those questions interesting is exactly the viewer who subscribes;
  one who doesn't was never going to stay.
- **Lines 4, 5, 6 and 7 are the owner's own words**, taken verbatim from POV bank
  answer 19. Nothing here is invented.
- **Every segment type already exists or is in build**, so this renders with the
  same pipeline as the episodes — no bespoke work.
