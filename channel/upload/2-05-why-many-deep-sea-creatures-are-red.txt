TITLE  (36/100)
why many deep sea creatures are red?

DESCRIPTION  (1509/5000)
------------------------------------------------------------------
Red light is absorbed near the ocean surface and does not illuminate deep water. A red animal therefore reflects little visible light at depth and can appear black, making red coloration an effective form of camouflage.
