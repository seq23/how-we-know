why deep sea creatures look so weird?

DESCRIPTION  (1657/5000)
------------------------------------------------------------------
Deep-sea creatures look unusual because darkness, crushing pressure, cold, and scarce food reward body plans that would be unnecessary near the surface—large mouths, light organs, soft tissues, unusual eyes, and energy-saving movement.
