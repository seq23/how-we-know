# Pronunciation probe

## Narration

Bioluminescence is the only light in the hadal zone. Challenger Deep lies at the southern end of the Mariana Trench, far below the bathypelagic layer.

The remotely operated vehicle Kaiko surveyed the trench floor. A cephalopod drifted past a hydrothermal vent, its arms catching the glow.

Researchers at Woods Hole, MBARI, and NOAA have each mapped parts of the trench. The bathymetry is still being refined.
